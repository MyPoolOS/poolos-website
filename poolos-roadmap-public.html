Shipped · v1.0
The foundation
A modern, stable Pentair ScreenLogic replacement. Everything you'd expect from a pool app — done properly.
Weather Guard
Prevents your heat pump from running when outdoor temperatures make it inefficient — protecting your equipment and your energy bill. Configurable thresholds, hysteresis logic, transparent status. Not available in any Pentair product.
Exclusive to PoolOS
Real-time dashboard
Pool temp, spa temp, air temp, heater status, and all your circuits — live, on one clean screen.
Instant controls
Toggle circuits, adjust setpoints, change light colors — the UI responds immediately, then verifies with your hardware in the background.
Pump diagnostics
Live RPM, wattage, and flow rate for your IntelliFlo variable speed pump.
Salt cell monitoring
Salt PPM, pool and spa chlorine output independently, super chlorinate status and duration.
Light color control
Every IntelliBrite color and show mode in one clean picker. No cycling through modes, no counting flashes.
UltraTemp diagnostics
Compressor state, ambient air temp, water temp at the unit, and error codes for your heat pump.
Schedule management
Add, edit, delete, and pause automation schedules from your phone. No touching the physical panel.
Remote access
Control your pool from anywhere via your existing Pentair relay. No new hardware. Works instantly.
Custom circuit names & icons
Rename your circuits, pick an icon, choose a color. Make the dashboard yours instead of whatever the installer named things.

Exploring · Future
The PoolOS hardware story
Pool equipment is capable of far more than any controller currently exposes — full RGB lighting, direct chiller control, deeper diagnostics. We're exploring hardware adapters that bypass the controller's limitations entirely. No timelines yet — we'll share more when we're ready.
Full RGB light control adapter
16 million colors. Any pool, any brand. A small Wi-Fi adapter that PoolOS controls directly — no manufacturer limit.
PoolOS Hub
A full controller replacement that talks directly to your equipment — surfacing all the data your hardware reports and enabling control your manufacturer never allowed.
