Overview

PoolOS ("the app") is a third-party controller interface for Pentair ScreenLogic-compatible pool systems, developed and maintained by Chad Baugh. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information — or more accurately, how it doesn't.

PoolOS is designed from the ground up to operate without collecting, transmitting, or storing any personal data. The app communicates exclusively with your Pentair ScreenLogic gateway on your local network.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. PoolOS does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your location or IP address
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Pool equipment data, settings, or usage history
  • App usage statistics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic information

How the app communicates

PoolOS connects directly to your Pentair ScreenLogic interface adapter over your home network. All communication stays between your iPhone and your controller — no intermediate servers, no cloud relay, no Pentair or third-party services involved.

When using PoolOS remotely (outside your home network), the app may use Pentair's ScreenLogic relay service to establish a connection to your controller. In this case, connection data passes through Pentair's infrastructure — this is a function of Pentair's own protocol, not something PoolOS adds or controls. PoolOS does not operate its own relay servers.

Local storage

Any preferences or settings you configure in PoolOS — such as your controller's IP address or app preferences — are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults system. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-party services

PoolOS does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social networking SDKs. There are no third-party services that collect data through PoolOS.

Children's privacy

PoolOS does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. As the app collects no data from any users, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date. Given that the app collects no data, material changes to this policy are unlikely.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how PoolOS works, you can reach us at privacy@mypoolos.app.
