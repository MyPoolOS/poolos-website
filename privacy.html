Overview

PoolOS ("the app") is a third‑party interface for Pentair ScreenLogic‑compatible pool systems, developed and maintained by Chad Baugh. You do not need a PoolOS account to control your pool.

Core use (default): commands and live status for your equipment go between your iPhone and your pool hardware on your network (ScreenLogic adapter or a PoolOS Bridge), or through Pentair’s ScreenLogic relay when you connect away from home. PoolOS does not send that pool‑control traffic to PoolOS‑operated servers.

In Settings, Weather Watch groups weather-related toggles; Storm Watch (severe weather alerts) is free and opt‑in. PoolOS Premium unlocks separate paid capabilities (billing through Apple). Only Storm Watch and Premium entitlements rely on PoolOS‑operated internet services — described in those sections.

Installer / operator tooling (servicing.mypoolos.app) is a separate browser portal (Cloudflare Access + Google sign‑in) used to run the business — it is not required to control your pool from the PoolOS app.

What we do not do

PoolOS does not include advertising SDKs, cross‑app tracking, or third‑party analytics/crash products (such as Firebase Analytics). We do not sell your personal information.

We do not ask for your name or email to use normal pool control.

Pool control & local network

On your home Wi‑Fi, PoolOS talks to your Pentair adapter or PoolOS Bridge directly. Operation data (temperatures, circuits, schedules) is not uploaded to PoolOS for that path.

When you use ScreenLogic’s remote connectivity, traffic may pass through Pentair’s systems under Pentair’s terms and privacy practices — the same pathway Pentair’s own apps use.

Storm Watch — free, opt‑in severe weather alerts

Storm Watch appears under Weather Watch in Settings. If you enable Storm Watch, the app registers for Apple Push Notifications and sends a small record to PoolOS‑operated backends (hosted on Cloudflare) so alerts can reach your phone. That can include:

  • Your device’s push token (handled by Apple’s push service)
  • Approximate pool location — from Location Services when you allow it, or coordinates you enter manually
  • Watch/critical radius settings you choose

The app may issue an occasional recovery HTTPS request to poll current alert state (for example after a long background). On the default PoolOS service, that request can include your push token inside the URL path over TLS — treated as a device identifier, not shared for advertising.

By default the app uses our hosted endpoint (weather.mypoolos.app). An advanced “Backend URL” field in Settings lets power users point Storm Watch at another compatible server; PoolOS is not responsible for that host’s privacy practices.

Weather and lightning data comes from reputable providers configured for the service (for example meteorological feeds). PoolOS uses that output only to evaluate alerts for your configured location — not for advertising.

PoolOS Premium & purchases (optional)

Premium subscriptions are purchased through Apple In‑App Purchase; Apple handles payment and subscription status for billing. PoolOS receives entitlement status via StoreKit on your device.

Paid features can include heat‑pump Weather Guard (under Weather Watch in Settings). Weather Guard evaluates outdoor air temperature from your pool equipment over your normal PoolOS connection (local network or Pentair relay) — it does not upload a separate pool‑telemetry feed to PoolOS for that purpose.

To support features like loyalty grants (for example introductory offers tied to installs or bridges) and redeemable codes, the app may contact entitlements.mypoolos.app (also Cloudflare‑hosted). We use a stable, random identifier stored on your device and a cryptographic hash derived with a salt — we do not receive your Apple ID or name from PoolOS code for those checks.

When you activate a PoolOS Bridge, the bridge software (not the iPhone UI) may send the same style of hashed identifier plus a bridge serial to entitlements so bundled Premium time can be credited and stacked per device policy.

Premium state may also be synced with Apple‑sent subscription events according to Apple’s Developer and App Store guidelines.

PoolOS Bridge setup (Bluetooth)

If you set up a bridge with Bluetooth, Wi‑Fi network names/passwords are sent over Bluetooth to your bridge locally for provisioning. PoolOS does not store your Wi‑Fi password on PoolOS servers.

When a bridge first connects to the internet successfully, it may call our entitlement service so Premium included with hardware is applied (see Premium above) — that path does not upload your pool control telemetry.

Data stored on your device

Preferences, caches, pairing state, controller addresses, credentials (including controller passwords stored with Apple Keychain‑style protection where applicable), and similar data stay on your iPhone unless a feature above explicitly sends minimal data outbound. Diagnostics use Apple’s standard on‑device logging for development support and are not shipped to PoolOS telemetry servers.

An optional debug log export (when you choose to share it) gathers recent on‑device logs and scrubs common push-token patterns before you send the file yourself — nothing is auto‑uploaded.

Retention & security

Server‑side records for Storm Watch and Premium entitlements exist only where needed to run those services (for example push registrations, entitlement rows, webhook audit). Reasonable safeguards protect those systems.

Children's privacy

PoolOS is a home‑pool utility, not directed at children. Parents and guardians decide whether minors use household equipment. Where optional cloud features transmit location or identifiers, guardians should supervise device permissions.

Changes to this policy

When we materially change practices, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the optional features after a change constitutes acceptance where applicable.
